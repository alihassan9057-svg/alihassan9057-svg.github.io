03 — What I do

Services

SEO Audit

A full technical, on-page and content audit with every finding evidenced and a ranked list of what to fix first. Usually the right place to start: you find out what is actually holding the site back before committing to a retainer.

Technical SEO

Crawl and indexation control, canonical and duplicate-content fixes, site architecture, internal linking, Core Web Vitals and page speed. The layer most audits name and few actually fix.

On-page & semantic SEO

Keyword research mapped to real search intent, page-level content optimization, topical structure, and schema markup that reflects what the page actually says.

E-commerce SEO

Shopify, Magento and WooCommerce stores: collection and product optimization, variant duplication, product schema, and content that feeds the buying funnel.

Local SEO

Google Business Profile built as a ranking asset, location-targeted page structures, review processes, and map pack visibility for service businesses and clinics.

Off-page & link building

Quality backlink acquisition, outreach, vendor evaluation and budget management, competitor gap analysis. I have run an entire agency's link budget.

Content strategy & blogs

Topic and intent mapping, editorial calendars, blog management, and content written to rank and convert rather than to fill a schedule.

Merchant Center & product feeds

Google Merchant Center setup and feed health: disapprovals, missing attributes, price and availability accuracy, and Shopping visibility for e-commerce catalogues.

Analytics & reporting setup

GA4, Google Tag Manager, Search Console and Looker Studio configured properly, with monthly KPI reporting that shows what the work is actually producing.

AI search & GEO

Generative Engine Optimization: answer-first content structure, structured data and entity consistency so your pages get cited in AI Overviews, ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity.

White label SEO for agencies

Audits, technical fixes, content and link building delivered under your brand, with reporting your clients can read. For agencies that need senior SEO capacity without hiring it.
